# analyze-retention: what keeps people watching

In plain words: look at **everything after the opening line**. What stops the
viewer from leaving, and how does the video end? The goal is to find the
techniques the account **repeats**, not to review one video.

## Step 0: read the playbook first (always)
Read `.claude/skills/instagram-playbook/SKILL.md`, mainly ideas 3, 5 and 6.

## Step 1: gather the transcripts
- **Text pasted in the chat:** use it directly.
- **A folder:** read every transcript file (`.txt`), one file per video.
- **Skip thin transcripts** (fewer than 15 words) and say how many you skipped.

If it is not clear where the transcripts are, ask only that.

## Step 2: walk through each video from start to finish
Look for these techniques and **quote the exact words** for each one you find:
- **Change of pace:** a switch of rhythm, topic or tone that wakes the viewer.
- **Open question:** something planted early and answered later.
- **Promise in parts:** an announced list or journey delivered step by step
  ("Day 1... Day 2...").
- **Second hook:** a new promise or twist in the middle.
- **Specific details:** numbers, names and concrete scenes that hold attention.
- **Reasons to react:** what invites a comment, a save or a share.
- **The ending:** does the last line echo the first? Is there a signature
  phrase? What does it ask the viewer to do?

## Step 3: find the patterns
- Which techniques does the account use **most**?
- Does it use lists or numbered steps to carry the viewer along?
- How does it usually **end**? Is it always the same?
- Where would a viewer probably **leave** (a slow middle, a long list)?
- What is the account's **signature way** of holding attention?

## Step 4: check your work
Confirm that every quote appears word for word in a transcript.

## What to deliver
Save as `analysis-retention.md` in the account's workspace folder, with:

1. **Evidence:** transcripts used and skipped.
2. **How this account holds attention, in short** (2 to 4 lines).
3. **Video by video:** beginning, middle, end, with the technique and the quote.
4. **Patterns:** the most used techniques and the usual ending.
5. **Reasons to react:** what it does to earn comments, saves and shares.
6. **Leaks:** where viewers probably leave, and why.
7. **Reusable techniques:** 2 to 4 moves taken from the account itself.

Always based on real quotes. No general advice like "keep the audience engaged".
